# Doing Research

Now that you have information on the services, you can research what possible vulnerabilities are out there. 

Sometimes, just searching a service or product and the version is enough. Other times, resources like [CVEDetails](https://cvedetails.com) can be useful to go through known vulnerabilities.

Places like [ExploitD](https://www.exploit-db.com/) can help you find exploits for vulnerable services. You can also search in Metasploit to see what exploits are available.
